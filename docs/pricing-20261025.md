**$39.99**, and Family Upgrade **$19.99**, with purchasing-power pricing in
other countries. All three product schedules were applied in App Store Connect

The policy uses the latest available 2023–2025 World Bank GNI per capita PPP:
`19.99 × sqrt(country / US)`, clamped to $4.99–$19.99 and rounded to the nearest
benchmark tier ($4.99, $6.99, $7.99, $9.99, $12.99, $14.99, $17.99, $19.99).
The product's Apple equalization maps that benchmark to a valid local price.
Family starts around twice Individual, with local adjustments when Apple
rounding would put it more than 1% above Individual plus Upgrade. Income-group
fallbacks apply when comparable data is missing. These are starting test prices,
not proven demand or sales optima.

Apple does not automatically maintain custom storefront prices for changes in
taxes or exchange rates. Monthly monitoring flags maintenance needs. Current
production paywalls use localized StoreKit prices; no app binary release is
needed. Development StoreKit catalogs were updated to the US prices.

A weekly Sunday 11:00 America/Puerto_Rico chat monitor compares purchases and
proceeds by product, country and purchasing-power cohort. It excludes the
October 25 transition day, stays quiet on insufficient or unchanged data, and
flags meaningful changes. App Store page views are acquisition context, not
in-app paywall conversion. The first full 28-day test window ends November 22.
The monitor does not change prices without further owner instruction.
