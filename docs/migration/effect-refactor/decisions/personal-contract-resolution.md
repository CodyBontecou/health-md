## Candidate field grammar and admission

The four accepted records define the positive shape of `health_fact`, `location_point`, `usage_aggregate` and `foreground_app_session`. Each record requires exactly `domain`, `payload_kind`, `payload_revision`, `record_id`, `lineage`, `time`, `payload`. `payload_revision` is the JSON integer 1 (boolean is invalid); legal domain/kind pairs are health/health_fact, location/location_point, device_usage/usage_aggregate and device_usage/foreground_app_session. No kind is inferred from field presence. All maps are closed to the keys in their selected shape; tagged unions permit only the selected arm. No unknown field is silently discarded or hashed after omission. An unsupported revision, field, domain/kind pair or number representation returns a fixed `unsupported_shape` or `invalid_exact_value` code before canonical ingestion. For this slice `health_fact.extensions` must be the empty array; nonempty native extensions require a separately reviewed source/extension schema before canonical ingestion. A separately admitted future extension cannot borrow revision 1.

`known` requires `value`; `unknown` requires a fixed `reason` and forbids `value`. Absence is field-specific: location `payload.altitude` and `payload.speed` use exactly `{state:"absent"}` with no reason/value/extra keys, preserving accepted record 1. Other admitted absent metadata arms use their accepted field shape with a fixed reason and no value; do not apply a blanket reason requirement across fields. Field-specific title states additionally allow `empty` (value is the empty string), `withheld`, `excluded`, `not_observed` (reason, no value). Missing required field is invalid, not implicit unknown. Empty string is not unknown. A title string is permitted only for classified nonbrowser apps with independent capture/detail/current-purpose grants; browser or unknown classification forbids acquisition and carrying the value. Location altitude/speed may be absent; coordinates are required. Unknown original device/install identity stays unknown, never a guessed descriptive identity.

Strings must contain Unicode scalar values, with no unpaired surrogate, NUL or normalization on ingest. Identity strings are nonempty, case-sensitive scalar sequences; no trimming, case folding or Unicode normalization defines equality. JSON duplicate keys and non-JSON numbers are rejected before map construction. Identifiers/reason tags in these synthetic fixtures have no public namespace authority. Reasons are closed fixed codes, not caller-supplied exception text. Array order is retained for record/evidence sequences; selector arrays represent sets, reject duplicates and are sorted lexically by scalar sequence only for canonical binding. Empty selectors select nothing, not everything. Missing required source revisions/statistic selectors/grant bindings must be explicitly supplied by the later operation request; they cannot be inferred from the combined example's abbreviated selector. Civil and instant selection are separate modes, never implicit intersection or union.

## Domain validity and authorized source evidence

Canonical latitude is finite and within [-90,90] degrees, longitude within [-180,180] degrees, both endpoints included; longitude is not wrapped. Accuracy is finite and >=0 meters. Altitude may be any finite meter value. Speed is finite and >=0 meters/second when known. Preserve negative zero bits as a valid mathematical zero. These are new candidate validation rules, not permission to rewrite source coordinates. Negative accuracy/speed from a native sentinel is retained as original authorized source evidence and projected to `unavailable` with fixed `native_invalid_accuracy` / `native_invalid_speed`, not positive zero or a valid measurement. Canonical unavailable sensor projection is a field-specific `{state:"unavailable",reason:...}` arm without `value`; absent remains separate. Out-of-range coordinate or nonfinite source value makes the canonical location projection `outside_canonical` with a fixed quality/status code. There is no valid point payload until both coordinates are valid. It must not silently vanish from a complete permitted native archive.

The additional candidate repository wrapper is exactly `{record_ref, canonical_status, reason_codes, source_evidence_ref}`. `canonical_status` is `valid`, `unavailable` or `outside_canonical`; `source_evidence_ref` is explicit known/unknown/absent, never inline raw data. Source evidence is an immutable, bounded, separately eligible source-record/artifact reference carrying original representation, source schema/revision and field-presence/quality, not a payload quarantine. Excluded browser/input material must never enter evidence, staging or archive. A source that cannot preserve an eligible invalid original has an explicit archive gap and cannot claim complete transfer. A canonical wire codec rejects nonfinite/out-of-range measurement payloads; a source-evidence adapter may retain the eligible original through the wrapper instead. These are separate boundaries, not conflicting acceptance of NaN in a canonical value. A normal canonical query omits invalid value payloads and reports only authorized fixed status/count; archive-purpose access to original evidence needs its own grant. No source hash authenticates that grant. Fixed additional codes in this resolution are `coordinate_out_of_range`, `nonfinite_source_value`, `native_invalid_accuracy`, `native_invalid_speed`, `clock_inconsistent`, `invalid_bucket`, `duration_outside_bucket`, `unsupported_shape`, `invalid_exact_value`, `candidate_limit_exceeded`, `record_identity_conflict`, `scope_binding_mismatch`, `aggregate_partial_overlap`, `scope_not_authorized` and `unknown_civil_scope`, `exact_projection_unrepresentable` and `restoration_fenced`; existing draft fixed reason codes retain their field-specific meaning. `reason_codes` is a duplicate-free lexically sorted array of these or the admitted draft codes.

Health units/statistics/native profile identities stay source-backed. `synthetic.health.*` is accepted only as fixture identity. No generic field range, coordinate conversion or usage reducer changes Apple v8/Android v4/v5 health bytes or aliases geographic distance to health distance.

Usage aggregate duration is finite >=0 seconds. A valid bucket has exact start < end. A duration larger than bucket width is not automatically clipped or rejected as corrupt: it is `outside_canonical` for the single-device/single-app elapsed-duration statistic unless independently admitted source semantics explain concurrency/device aggregation; original evidence remains retained and no person-total is fabricated. Observed session start/end must be ordered start <= end; zero and all observed short durations are retained. Reversed boundaries or negative source duration are clock-inconsistent source facts, not valid sessions. Duration disagreement with exact rational boundary difference is `outside_canonical` only for the private `synthetic_observation` basis, whose fixture asserts exact wall-clock arithmetic. Ordinary native Date/Double subtraction or epoch conversion may round: binary64 0.1 to binary64 1.1 can produce measured binary64 1.0 while the exact endpoint difference is 1+3/2^55. This alone is not clock inconsistency and cannot reject a future native source. A proposed `native_source_arithmetic` basis must preserve original endpoint bits/epochs, duration bits/unit, source algorithm/revision, subtraction/conversion representation and available source arithmetic provenance; it remains unadmitted until independently qualified semantic rules exist. No epsilon repair, rounded endpoint replacement or invented monotonic basis is permitted. Reversed endpoints/negative duration remain separately classified source evidence. Keep original boundaries/duration and no repaired session/zero duration. A future monotonic duration basis requires independent source semantics. Unknown/open boundaries remain uncertain facts; they cannot claim exact observed session duration. Aggregate bucket edges never become session edges.

## Stable identity and equality

The logical source-record key is the structured tuple `(dataset_id, source_id, source_contract_revision, device_state_and_value, installation_state_and_value, original_identity_namespace, original_identity_value, record_revision)`. Unknown device/install values use explicit tagged unknowns but are not equivalence proof across sources. If native original identity is unavailable, namespace is `import_record`, value is a structured `(immutable_artifact_id, original_logical_partition_id, source_record_ordinal)` and original native identity stays unknown. Artifact ID binds complete original eligible artifact bytes plus format/revision; native artifact IDs may be separately recorded. The logical partition and ordinal are fixed by the original artifact parser, before transport rechunking. If the source format has no partitions, use literal partition `root` and ordinal in original source record order. Repacked/repartitioned transports carry this original key; they never replace it with new page/partition ordinal. An artifact containing excluded material cannot be copied/hashed wholesale to establish this identity. Eligibility must be decidable from field/class metadata before observing excluded values; otherwise reject that import route. A separately issued eligible artifact is a different explicitly related artifact, not a claim to have preserved the excluded original. Missing a stable original enumeration means import identity is unknown and automatic idempotent merging is refused. Unknown native identity is never manufactured from time/location/app similarity.

Dataset revision and source snapshot/grant/import/upload timestamps bind requests and evidence, not a new original record on every retry. A record ID is a collision-safe injective encoding/registry of that structured key; string concatenation without length/type framing is prohibited. Native UUID identity retains its namespace and original representation. Different installations or source contracts remain separate until an explicitly reviewed migration relation exists.

Two distinct digests have distinct purposes. The artifact digest binds original authorized artifact bytes, including formatting/order, and is not source-record semantic equality. Source-record equality compares the complete lossless eligible source-record field vector at the same key: all typed original fields, source bits, unknown/absent distinctions, original quality/native extension evidence and original field order where meaningful. Transport whitespace, paging, derived canonical projection, imported_at/uploaded_at and recipient metadata are excluded. Missing field versus explicit unknown remains different. A new unknown source field invalidates that source schema/admission rather than disappearing before equality. Permitted raw original evidence remains linked for audit. Excluded fields are rejected before observation; neither equality nor archive completeness licenses collecting them. No hash computed after lossy normalization proves complete equality.

Same key/equal complete eligible source record is one idempotent original with additional immutable import evidence. Same key/different complete source record is `record_identity_conflict`; never last-write-wins. A source revision/correction is a separately identified relation, not an exception allowing silent replacement. Hash collision handling requires full exact field-vector comparison or authenticated collision-resistant binding with independently qualified storage; synthetic hash labels alone are not proof.

## Exact comparison and selection

Convert finite binary64 bits to a mathematical rational using sign s, exponent E and fraction F: subnormal E=0 is `(-1)^s * F * 2^-1074`; normal is `(-1)^s * (2^52+F) * 2^(E-1023-52)`. Negative zero compares as zero while retained bits remain distinct. Add exact integer 978307200 seconds for Apple reference 2001. Unix binary64 adds zero. Seconds+nanos compares as `(epoch_seconds*1000000000+nanoseconds)/1000000000` using arbitrary precision integers. Compare/equal by exact integer cross multiplication, no Number/Date/ISO/millisecond conversion, epsilon or rounded nanos. This comparison representation is transient mathematics, never a claim of source nanosecond accuracy. A lossy canonical time conversion is refused; retain native bits/epoch. Bounds and intermediate integer sizes are limited by finite binary64/i64 grammar and bounded input; no unbounded decimal acceptance.

Point membership is `query_start <= instant < query_end`. Query intervals require start < end. Civil membership uses inclusive valid owner dates with matching source owner-rule evidence; unknown owner dates stay unknown and strict requests fail, allow-partial reports omission. Scope/grant checks precede even evidence materialization and recur at each page/commit under the current suppression frontier.

An aggregate is selected only when its entire half-open bucket is contained in the requested interval. Disjoint or touching buckets are excluded. A partially overlapping bucket cannot supply an exact requested total: strict exact selection fails `aggregate_partial_overlap` without a deliverable; allow-partial omits it and reports incomplete authorized coverage. An explicit separate `whole_bucket_context` projection may include the original whole duration plus original bucket and `extends_beyond_selection:true`, only when time/detail grants authorize the entire bucket. It contributes nothing to requested-interval totals. Never estimate proportionally, clip aggregate duration, stretch ten minutes to one hour or silently sum hourly/daily/device overlaps. Unresolved projection precedence returns separate originals and no combined total; a reviewed overlap group/revision policy is required for deduplication.

For an observed consistent session, overlap is start < query_end and end > query_start; zero-length sessions are selected as points. The original session is immutable. A requested clipped projection is a new derivation with separately identified original key, exact intersection, algorithm/revision/selection inputs and duration basis; it is not a new raw session. Derive duration only from valid exact boundaries; source-inconsistent/unknown boundaries yield no exact clipping. The mathematical intersection duration may be a rational that fits neither finite binary64 nor the current integer scalar grammar. Exact clipping then requires an independently reviewed rational or exact-ticks derivation schema carrying numerator/denominator or qualified tick scale, algorithm and lineage; that schema is not admitted here. Without it reject the projection with fixed `exact_projection_unrepresentable`, never round. The literal 4-second clipping example is representable as a canonical integer second; it does not admit arbitrary rational duration output. Independent grants govern whether original whole boundaries may be revealed or only the derived permitted interval. No automatic correlation of simultaneous location/health/usage records follows selection.

## Current suppression, correction and restoration frontier

Candidate repository visibility is evaluated against a current monotonic suppression revision per dataset/record scope plus current grants. Append-only events have independent event IDs/revisions, target keys, reason codes and explicit known/unknown event times. Candidate event shape is exactly `{event_id, event_kind, revision, target_keys, reason_code, event_time, related_event_ids, authority_ref}`; kind is `correction`, `tombstone`, `delete`, `revoke`, `restore` or `reversal`, revision is canonical u64 allocated by the accepted repository authority, IDs/targets are immutable structured keys and time is an explicit tagged state. Same revision/different event is a conflict; timestamps never decide precedence. Correction value/evidence references need an independently reviewed payload schema before an actual correction transaction is admitted. Query/export applies latest accepted frontier before reading/materializing, for every page and immediately before commit; a cursor with stale frontier or grant revision fails `scope_binding_mismatch` and staged results remain invisible. No grant revision is accepted merely because it is in the request.

A tombstone suppresses the targeted original and dependent current projections/derivations. A correction links to the original and can replace its current projection only under explicit reviewed revision precedence; it does not mutate original bytes or bypass tombstone suppression. Two unordered/conflicting corrections are a conflict, never a guessed winner. Undo appends a new reversal event. Delete additionally records a durable deletion fence covering old imports/snapshots/derivations; replay, outbox retry, backup restore or regrant cannot resurrect fenced data. Revocation suppresses operations/details immediately without pretending it physically deletes every retained source; capture/query/export/recipient enrollment remain separate controls.

Restoration in this resolution is limited to reversible local tombstone/correction/reversal events for nondeleted, currently eligible evidence, under explicit reviewed restoration intent and current source-purpose/detail/destination grants. It cannot override accepted deletion, authoritative revocation, purged evidence or a current external suppression journal/frontier. Delete/revoke fences remain dominant regardless of a later restore event, Undo, backup, regrant, changed name or local revision. A separately user-selected future contract would need to qualify a genuinely new source and its policy before any fenced-lineage restoration; this resolution admits none. Newly captured independently identified records under current authority are not resurrection of old fenced records and may not rename/rekey old evidence to evade suppression. Physical purge, retention exemptions, cryptographic deletion, crash-safe fences and permitted reversible local restoration remain later repository/cloud proofs. Separately granted archive audit may include locally tombstoned historical evidence only when current deletion/retention policy expressly permits it; archive access cannot bypass deletion/revocation or the authoritative external frontier. Already downloaded recipient copies are not recalled; future reads/commit/publication stop under current grants and suppression. CLOUD-FAULT-MODEL continues to own invisible staging, stream authentication, SQL/object ledger, deletion replay and recipient-copy limits.
