# Health.md $19.99 Lifetime Price Experiment

## Status

- Results status: in progress (window opened 2026-09-17)
- Product: `com.codybontecou.obsidianhealth.unlock` (individual), `…unlock.family`, `…unlock.family.upgrade`
- ASC change date: **2026-09-17** (schedule created same day; base territory USA)

This note continues the lifetime-price series started by ISO-294
($9.99 → $14.99). Unlike ISO-294, the dates and prior results are recorded
at the start of the window, not after.

## $14.99 window results (2026-06-01 → 2026-09-16)

Sources: D1 `health-md-pricing-analytics` (80,511 events) and ASC commerce
reports (monthly + daily instances). D1 captures ~93% of Apple's units.

- Activated users: 1,542 · paywall views: 2,184 · purchases: 304
- Conversion per paywall view: ~13.9% (pre-change $9.99: 24.0%, launch-cohort biased)
- **Proceeds per activated user: $2.78** (pre: ~$1.45) — **+92%**
- Revenue per paywall view essentially flat (~$2.01 → ~$1.96): the 1.5×
  price almost exactly offset a ~40% per-view conversion decline
- Net proceeds (all territories): Jun $1,240 · Jul $1,558 · Aug $979
- Decay inside the window: proceeds/activated fell Jul $3.35 → Aug $1.92 →
  Sep ~$1.69, driven by the free-limit raise 3 → 10 (Jul 27) halving
  paywall exposure per activated user, plus diluted August traffic
  (activation rate 67% → 43%). Not a pricing signal.

## $19.99 test window

- Window: 2026-09-17 → decision no earlier than 2026-10-08 (≥3 full weeks),
  first read ~2026-09-24
- Confounds in window: the soft upgrade prompts (3rd/7th export) and the
  shortened onboarding + post-onboarding paywall ship in the first app
  release after 2026-09-17; until that build reaches users, the old
  onboarding shows the new price.

## Metrics and decision rule

Decision metric (same as ISO-294): **net proceeds per activated user**, with
quality gates.

- **Keep $19.99 if**: proceeds/activated ≥ $1.92 (August's $14.99 level) and
  conversion per paywall view ≥ ~11%, with no material refund/review/support
  degradation.
- **Revert to $14.99 if**: proceeds/activated < ~$1.50, or conversion per
  paywall view craters below ~10% without a compensating revenue gain, or
  quality gates degrade.
- **Probe $24.99 next only if** this window is cleanly healthy.

Query inputs: `pricing_export_preview_generated`, `pricing_export_succeeded`,
`pricing_paywall_shown`, `pricing_purchase_finished`, and the new
`pricing_upgrade_prompt_*` events; ASC commerce reports for proceeds.
