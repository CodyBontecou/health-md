# Health.md outreach prospects

Checked 2026-10-26. Public-source research only; no pitches sent, listings submitted, or contacts added to a mailing list. Ranking reflects editorial fit and a concrete story, not measured domain authority or a promise of a followed backlink. This companion to the [backlink audit](2026-10-26-healthmd-backlink-audit.md) follows the dated research-note convention in `docs/experiments/`.

See the [backlink audit and its sample limits](2026-10-26-healthmd-backlink-audit.md) before interpreting the prospect list as a list of new referring domains.

## Product and evidence boundaries

The current [Health.md homepage](https://healthmd.app/) positions the product around local-first health data exports to Markdown, JSON, CSV and Obsidian Bases, scheduled device exports, and paired-device CLI/MCP queries. Its scheduling caveat says the iPhone must be unlocked. Keep these boundaries in pitches; do not present the single-owner cloud pilot as a generally available service. For AI stories, distinguish local file storage from the privacy behavior of whichever AI service the reader chooses.

The prospects below are **no-known-link prospects**, not proven new referring domains. Public search did not establish comprehensive backlink coverage. A page not mentioning the app does not prove the publisher has never linked elsewhere. Reconcile these domains against a complete backlink export before counting acquired links as new domains. Author emails are included only where an official source exposed them; otherwise use the linked official contact route.

## Start with three specific assets

1. A selected-data export demonstration for TidBITS: choose one date range and a few metrics, show the output, and contrast the task with working through a complete archive. Use fictional data, not a journalist's personal health data.
2. A fictional Obsidian starter vault for The Sweet Setup and This Week in Obsidian: exported files, a readable weekly review and working charts. Include the export setup and the iPhone-unlock scheduling condition.
3. A short MacStories automation demo: input selections, an actual successful run, output files and a useful destination. Make every claim reproducible in the current release.

Pitch the useful result and let the publication decide whether coverage and a link are warranted. Do not ask for dofollow tags or exact-match commercial anchor text. A placement can be worthwhile for relevant readers even if it does not increase DR.

## Existing discovery and exclusions

- **Obsidian Stats already has a Health.md Visualizations listing:** [existing plugin page](https://www.obsidianstats.com/plugins/health-md). Treat it as listing maintenance/possible URL verification, not a new prospect. The listing is not proof of a direct followed link to healthmd.app.
- **Obsidian Roundup is excluded.** Its old domain redirected away from the expected publication when opened. The [This Week in Obsidian introduction](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/this-week-in-obsidian-a-tuesday-newsletter-for-obsidian-users/110776) also describes the older newsletter as apparently inactive. Do not pitch an unverified recycled domain.
- **Direct competitor export apps are excluded.** Their feature pages establish market interest but generally provide a weaker editorial reason to recommend a competing app.
- No prospect DR, monthly audience, acceptance probability, personal email pattern, or link attribute has been invented. Those fields remain unmeasured.

## Publisher requirements that affect execution

- [TidBITS](https://tidbits.com/about/contact/) explicitly rejects guest posts, sponsored posts, link insertions and press releases. The proposed action is a carefully relevant product test/story idea tied to Adam's own article; do not turn it into an SEO-link request.
- [MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/about/) says it tests reviewed apps itself and does not accept paid reviews. Supply review access and a working workflow; retain editorial independence.
- [MacSparky](https://www.macsparky.com/about/) explicitly rejects paid-link deals and cross-posting pitches. Keep the note short and useful to David's demonstrated interests.

## Measurement

Before outreach, record each prospect domain's existing backlink status from a complete export if available. Then track contact date, response, published URL, direct destination URL, link attribute, relevant visits and App Store/CLI conversions. Check DR monthly as a secondary measure; this research does not support a predicted points increase.
