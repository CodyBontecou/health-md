# Wear OS phases 1–7 completion audit

This is the prompt-to-artifact audit for the Wear OS implementation worktree. A passing build,
manifest count, validator, emulator run, or substitute signature is scoped evidence only. The
project is not complete while any row is `BLOCKED` or `UNVERIFIED`.

## Concrete completion criteria

1. A separately buildable Wear companion, private contract module, Tile, real Data Layer policy,
   and separate Play artifact exist and are compatible with the phone package/listing.
2. A bounded/versioned daily-aggregate DTO is produced by the phone from granted Health Connect
   data, including correctly mapped oxygen, with fixtures and rejection tests.
3. Phone/watch synchronization is durable, permission-aware, ordered, clearable, scheduled only
   when eligible, manually controllable, and represented truthfully in phone settings.
4. The watch dashboard uses only its atomic no-backup cache and covers setup, partial, stale,
   expired, unavailable, mismatch, refresh, navigation, round scrolling, and design tokens.
5. Exactly two Tiles and ten Android-native complication providers render truthful local data and
   no-data states.
6. Offline/privacy/accessibility/localization behavior is proven, and the documented Pixel and
   Samsung battery/OEM protocol passes.
7. Emulator CI and paired phone/Wear AAB release automation pass; exact-release Play screenshots,
   production signing, form-factor recognition, closed-track installs/upgrades/deletion, and exact
   paired promotion are proven.
