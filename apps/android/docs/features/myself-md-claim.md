# Complimentary myself.md access

The claim feature is **disabled by default** on Apple and Android. Settings entries and claim deep links are gated; disabled links do not read purchases or start verification. Android: build with `-PMYSELF_CLAIM_ENABLED=true` (or the equivalent build environment variable). Google Play purchase availability is still required; F-Droid stays unavailable. Enabling requires a new build; this is not a remote or user-facing toggle.

Google Play purchasers can open Settings → **Claim myself.md access**, or the exact `healthmd://myself/claim` link. The screen first explains the transfer of purchase proof, one-account rule, and browser confirmation. Opening it does not query purchases, upload proof, sign in or claim an entitlement. F-Droid exposes no entry, route or Play billing dependency; included/free/grandfathered Android access is not paid purchase evidence.

**Verify purchase and continue** queries the existing process-scoped Play Billing client for a fresh completed `health_md_premium_lifetime` INAPP purchase. Pending, missing, wrong-product or empty-token results cannot proceed. Cached unlock flags, debug overrides and installation dates do not supply evidence. The action never launches a checkout or consumes a purchase.

Only `{ app: "health.md", store: "android", kind: "iap", proof: purchaseToken }` is sent to the fixed HTTPS `https://myself.md/api/migration/proof` endpoint. No health/location/export data, account identifier or client eligibility assertion is sent. Redirects are disabled and responses are bounded. The returned handoff must use the same HTTPS origin, exact `/claim` path, no credentials or query, and a 43-character opaque ticket fragment. Purchase tokens and tickets are never logged or persisted. Closing cancels in-flight work; an already-issued unredeemed ticket expires on the server without granting access.

The server independently verifies the purchase with Google, checks completed/unconsumed ownership, and issues a short-lived ticket. The browser asks the purchaser to sign in, inspect the receiving account and explicitly confirm. A purchase already claimed by another account is rejected; same-account retries are safe. A browser launch means **awaiting user**, never completed access. Sign in to myself.md with the same account after confirming. `get_lifetime_access` exposes the existing owner-scoped guide and entitlement read; an agent cannot confirm or grant itself access. Account/store authentication remain user handoffs. The claim flow shares the existing backend; it changes no health export, CLI protocol or schema.

Errors stay on the disclosure with a retry button. Every retry queries fresh Play evidence; no previous purchase token or ticket is replayed from disk. Rapid taps coalesce, missing browsers report failure, and cold/warm links open the same disclosure without skipping its user action. URI credentials, ports, query strings, fragments and extra paths are rejected. Claim links are excluded from Shared Setup document import.

## Verification

Focused Android unit tests cover proof selection, explicit action, cancellation, duplicate taps, retry, browser failure, HTTPS request shape, bounded replies, redirects and untrusted claim links. Native UI tests cover disclosure, retry/cancel, busy and awaiting-user states, plus actual cold/warm route handling. The shared capability inventory and Apple/Android consumer assertions record source availability, not store distribution.

Successful production purchase verification, account selection and redemption require a consenting real Google Play purchaser. Fixture handoffs do not prove production eligibility. No Play release or real-account redemption is implied by local validation.

On 18 October 2026, the Pixel 7 ran all 13 targeted native cases successfully, including ten constrained/large-font/dark/RTL viewports and actual cold/warm routes. The 22 Play and 10 F-Droid unit cases, both Android lint tasks, shared contract validator and the actual Apple capability XCTest passed. The updated Play Debug app was installed without clearing app data. Minimal receipts are retained in ignored `.pi/myself-claim-android/`. The optional Argent recording could not be accepted: screenshots showed the disclosure, while its Android devtools tree continued to report onboarding even after an app restart. The aborted recording is retained only as diagnostics, not as a passing regression flow. Native Compose tests provide the navigation and accessibility evidence.
