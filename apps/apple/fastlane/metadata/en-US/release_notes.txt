Health.md 3.0.5 turns your Apple Health data into a clinician-ready report without sending it anywhere.

New
• Create 7-, 30-, 90-day, or custom-range reports with 11 focused health metrics.
• Preview summaries and detailed readings, then export an accessible Letter or A4 PDF.
• Scan a Direct CLI pairing QR in the app to connect more quickly.
• Use the fully localized Mac app in German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Dutch, Brazilian Portuguese, or Simplified Chinese.

Privacy
• Report generation stays on your device. Temporary files remain private until you explicitly share or save them.

Improved
• Missing, partial, and unavailable readings are explained clearly so reports do not imply data that is not there.
• The Mac dashboard makes connection, destination, export activity, and settings clearer, with localized labels that wrap cleanly.
