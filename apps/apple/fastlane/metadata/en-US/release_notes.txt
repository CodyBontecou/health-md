Health.md 3.0.3 makes large exports clearer and more reliable.

Changed
• Clinical Health Records access is temporarily unavailable. Ordinary Apple Health metrics and lossless source-sample exports remain available.

Improved
• New installs begin with faster summary-only exports. Lossless Health Records remains available as an opt-in for complete canonical source archives.
• Preview and Export Data remain visible on iPad, and empty Apple Health libraries now show clear date-range and permission guidance instead of a generic error.
• On iOS, the exported-file success view now appears only after the entire multi-file export finishes.
• Connected iPhone-to-Mac exports recover more reliably and use less memory while preparing large summaries.
• Weekly, monthly, and yearly roll-up estimates and progress now reflect their complete calendar windows.
• The Mac app no longer accesses Keychain during ordinary launch; encrypted context status loads only when requested.
