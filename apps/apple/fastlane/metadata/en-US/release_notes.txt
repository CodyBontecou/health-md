Health.md 3.2 gives you more control over exports.

New
• Stop running exports — one banner owns export progress, and Stop cancels the running scheduled, Shortcut, or manual export while completed dates stay completed and schedules remain enabled.
• Export Data Detail now separates Detailed Time-Series from Lossless Health Records, so you can export per-sample data without the much larger canonical archive. Summary, Detailed Time-Series, and Lossless presets live in the Export tab and export profiles.

Fixed
• Direct CLI Access reconnects automatically after your computer sleeps, the Mac app quits, or the network changes.
• Folders on "On My iPhone" storage keep their saved selection across restarts, so automatic exports and Shortcuts keep working.
