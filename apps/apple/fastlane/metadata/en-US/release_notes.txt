Health.md 3.4.0 introduces Share My Setup, makes daily scheduled exports dependable, and reports export results truthfully.

Added
• Share My Setup (Settings → Configuration): package your export profiles — metric selection, formats, and naming — into one small portable file and hand it to your other device or a friend. Review exactly what will change, apply as a transactional Add or Replace, and Undo once. Files carry no health data or credentials; imported profiles stay blocked until you rebind their destination, and older v1 setup files are no longer accepted.

Fixed
• Daily scheduled exports no longer skip every other day — each run now exports the prior day’s data.
• Export History now shows the real number of generated files for scheduled, Shortcut, and manual runs, and reports truthful outcomes for partial exports.
• A workout whose optional WorkoutKit plan can’t be decoded no longer marks the whole export “Partial” — all samples still export and the omission appears as an informational note.
• Retry Export now sits at the top of the export detail sheet, with long failed-date lists capped.
