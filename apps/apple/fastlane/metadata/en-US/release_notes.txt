Health.md 3.4.1 makes setup sharing, scheduled exports, and accessibility more dependable.

• Share My Setup lets you move export profiles between devices in one small file, review changes before applying them, and undo once. Shared files contain no health data or credentials.
• Scheduled profiles now refresh the full lookback you chose on every run. Optional Today Refresh can update today’s in-progress export every 3, 6, or 12 hours.
• Export History reports generated files and outcomes more clearly, with retries and informational notes easier to reach.
• VoiceOver, keyboard focus, touch targets, scrolling, and large-text layouts are improved across onboarding, export setup, metrics, schedules, and connection screens.
