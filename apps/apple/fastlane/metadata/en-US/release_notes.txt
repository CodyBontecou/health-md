Health.md 4.0 is a complete redesign for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

• Archive: a calendar that fills in as you export, with a page for each day showing the file each automation wrote.
• Needs attention: problems such as an unavailable folder, an offline Mac or a failing endpoint are listed with their fix.
• Automations read as plain sentences, with a live preview of the file they will write. Run, duplicate or pause each one, or use it in Shortcuts.
• Export now: yesterday, the last 7 or 30 days, or any dates.
• New setup: choose what to track, preview your past week as notes, and save them. Your first export is free.
• Choose whether each night's sleep counts on the day it starts or the day you wake.
• Set detail per category, for example detailed sleep with summarized heart rate.
• Schedules can include today, and folders can be organized by ISO week.
• New Archive widget and refreshed widgets, Live Activity, Mac menu bar and Watch app.
• Saved API endpoints can be shared across automations.
• Fixes for custom date ranges, daily-note frontmatter fields and empty-day exports.
