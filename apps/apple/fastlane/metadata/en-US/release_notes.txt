Health.md 3.0.4 now feels at home in more languages and makes first-time setup clearer.

New
• Full app localization for German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Dutch, Brazilian Portuguese, and Simplified Chinese.
• Localized guidance across export, scheduling, formatting, preview, tracking, and Mac destination flows.

Improved
• A clearer onboarding path gets you from Apple Health and folder setup to your first export.
• Scheduled exports share the same free-export allowance as manual exports.
• You can choose whether exports include the Health.md data dictionary.
• Privacy and analytics explanations now clarify what limited product events contain—and what they never include.
