Health.md 3.5.0 adds weekly and yearly subscriptions alongside one-time Lifetime access on iPhone and iPad.

• Eligible subscriptions include a 7-day free trial, then renew automatically at the displayed price unless canceled.
• Existing Lifetime purchases and grandfathered access are preserved, including eligible Family Sharing.
• Active access is required before setup and exports on iPhone and iPad. The free macOS companion is unchanged.
• Purchase status and scheduling guidance distinguish subscription and Lifetime access.
