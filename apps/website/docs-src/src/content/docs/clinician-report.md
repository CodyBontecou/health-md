---
title: "Clinician Report"
description: "Create a private, descriptive PDF on iPhone or Android with explicit coverage and no Health.md health-data cloud."
---

Clinician Report turns an inclusive range into a PDF that you preview and explicitly share. Reading, normalization, and rendering happen on the device.

<div class="availability available"><strong>Available on iPhone and Android</strong><p>Open Settings → Clinician Report. It is not a scheduled export format or a Mac report workflow.</p></div>

1. Choose 7, 30, or 90 days, or a custom range.
2. Select measurements and summary or detailed tables.
3. Optionally enter a display name used only in this report.
4. Preview, then share or save the PDF.

The 11 areas are blood pressure, heart rate and resting heart rate, weight, glucose, oxygen, respiratory rate, temperature, sleep, steps, and workouts. The report preserves coverage and never claims that missing data proves an event did not occur.

It does not diagnose, calculate risk, or recommend treatment. Only trustworthy blood-pressure pairs are included. Temporary files remain private until your explicit share action.
