Account · Pricing

Unlock & Paywall

One-time Full Access purchase, no subscription. Unlock unlimited exports, scheduling, Mac destination workflows, and Shortcuts support.

Pricing

  • Full Access is a one-time StoreKit purchase shown inside the App Store purchase sheet.
  • No subscription and no recurring charge.
  • The live local price is shown by Apple before purchase.
  • No server-side account. The unlock is tied to your Apple ID StoreKit transaction.

What Full Access unlocks

  • Unlimited exports.
  • Scheduled background exports.
  • Mac destination workflows.
  • Shortcuts intents.

Restore a previous purchase

Tap Restore Purchase on the paywall. The app queries StoreKit for any prior purchases tied to the signed-in Apple ID. This is the path to use after reinstalling the app or moving to a new device.

Refunds & support

Refunds go through Apple — open a request at reportaproblem.apple.com. For app-side support, email cody@isolated.tech.
