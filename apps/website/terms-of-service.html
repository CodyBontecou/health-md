5. What Health.md does

Depending on platform and availability, Health.md can:

  • read authorized Apple HealthKit, WorkoutKit, Android Health Connect, and Health Connect medical-record data;
  • export selected data as Markdown, Obsidian, CSV, JSON, FHIR/provider-native, and other documented formats;
  • write to a chosen local or cloud-backed folder, vault, document provider, paired Mac or CLI, or user-configured API endpoint;
  • run manual, scheduled, Shortcut, Android automation, direct, and resumable export workflows subject to platform limits;
  • answer scoped typed queries and return factual values, provenance, coverage, missingness, evidence, or charts through supported CLI and MCP clients;
  • connect to supported health providers after authorization and pass provider records to the device;
  • use limited non-health infrastructure for scheduling, purchases, product analytics, campaign attribution, provider authorization, website delivery, and support as described in the Privacy Policy.

We do not collect or store your health data on Health.md servers. Health data can still leave your device when you choose a synced folder, paired client, provider, agent or model service, API endpoint, or another destination. That destination controls its copy.
