# Public Cloud MVP service, native apps and dashboard feature backlog

**Status: proposed intentionally unbacked MVP, not design or launch approval.** The owner has explicitly declined backups, so this backlog no longer promises to retire permanent-loss disclosure or deliver the earlier recoverable-service objective. It does not authorize backups of the live pilot or MVP, provisioning, deployment, signup, migration, new mobile behavior, or removal of the pilot warning.

The [design interview](production-design-interview.md) records the owner's product direction, selected v1 backend-decryption trust model and remaining design choices. The [rollout plan](multi-user-production-rollout-plan.md), [completion audit](production-readiness-audit.md) and [proposed ADR-0008](../../../docs/architecture/adr-0008-multi-user-healthmd-cloud-production.md) still describe the earlier invite-first/MCP-deferred architecture and evidence; they need a reviewed revision for the new target, not a presumed approval. Findings below come from checked-in source and documentation, not a fresh inspection of live infrastructure.

## Desired user outcome

A US adult can sign up without an invitation, explicitly select the native Cloud export target, send chosen JSON exports to the isolated service, claim the selected 10 GB lifetime-linked benefit if eligible or choose a monthly/annual decimal 1 GB, 50 GB, 100 GB or 1 TB plan through native checkout (web may follow if qualified), authorize remote agent access, verify what Cloud received, understand billing/quota/retention/permanent-loss risk, browse/download original evidence, secure their account, and stop access or delete it. Health.md's MVP compute/storage must remain US-only; authorized agent/model providers are a separate disclosed boundary. The archive is intentionally unbacked: data, metadata or service-key loss may permanently destroy access. No formal recovery timing targets, archive restoration or removal of the loss warning is promised. Safe operation, account recovery and truthful public-MVP disclosure are distinct from archive/key recovery.

## Recorded product direction

- First public MVP: **self-service signup for US individual adults**, with explicit US-residence/18+/own-data self-attestation rather than collected ID documents. This is not verified identity/residence or a privacy/platform exemption; no invite-only, family, organization or clinical-account product.
- Archive dataset: **only successfully received exports**, never a claimed complete phone-health backup.
- **Remote MCP is essential at launch** and the reason for the Cloud environment. Required client targets: ChatGPT, ChatGPT Dots, Claude app **and Claude Code**, **Muse from Meta's consumer personal-agent app**, Grok Bot, Pi Durable and Pi with local/remote MCP capability. Maintained adapters are allowed. Existing Muse pilot connector work is documented, and the owner believes it works; checked-in evidence does not establish a confirmed in-app read or public multi-user qualification. Each advertised surface requires real qualification, not a vendor-API or Muse Code substitute.
- **Unattended archive access:** queries must work without the exporting device or a personal browser being online. New exports still need the device; the Cloud endpoint's availability does not make an offline client process run jobs.
- **Full archive access by default:** explicit approval of a new All connection covers every original retained field/byte, including newly received exports while the grant remains authorized. Name the actual client/provider/runtime and credential-sharing boundary. Do not upgrade existing narrow credentials silently.
- **Authorized backend decryption is accepted for v1:** service-managed per-account encryption with a separately isolated authorized reader. Health.md can process authorized plaintext; this is **not zero-knowledge**. Exact US key custody still needs review; no independent historical-key recovery is selected.
- **As many Agent connections as users need**, with service-configurable future limits. The pilot's ten-active-grant cap is not public product policy; abuse, concurrency and response bounds remain. **Shared credentials between Bots/services are allowed**, superseding mandatory per-Bot isolation. Revoke the shared grant, not an allegedly isolated Bot; another active owner-authorized grant may remain usable by those same Bots.
- **Authorization lasts until revoked.** Q34 selects qualified OAuth refresh where supported and reviewed, protected until-revoked read-only bearer credentials where needed; no public implementation is qualified yet. Lower limits restrict new grants only, not existing grants or their reconnect/refresh/rotation. Account disablement, deletion and independent security revocation remain effective.
- **Three launch tasks:** discover uploaded coverage/gaps; compare metrics, sleep and workouts across periods; retrieve exact retained source evidence. Q31 accepts a published, reviewed Apple/Android metric/sleep/workout support matrix, not typed coverage of every field. Preserve proven semantics and honest missing/unavailable states; every accepted original byte remains readable under All, without promising interpretation of every format.
- **Provider handling:** Q32 accepts reviewed/disclosed provider-default retention/training with explicit consent only where platform/privacy review permits, plus guided opt-out where available. No downstream deletion/geography guarantee, blanket training permission or reused pilot consent.
- **Native Cloud export target:** Q27/Q41–Q42 select first-class iOS and Android Google Play targets with passkey enrollment, explicit Cloud/loss/data-scope consent, secure per-device revocable write-only authority and a verified one-day receipt. F-Droid first-class Cloud integration is excluded; macOS Cloud uploads are outside initial scope. Preserve local/Connected Mac/custom-API behavior, selected permissions/detail, frozen retry bindings and F-Droid's existing full access. The channel exception needs explicit product/capability review, not an invented OS limitation.
- **Paid and included storage:** Q35–Q36 select monthly/annual **1 GB, 50 GB, 100 GB and 1 TB** plans; Q44 selects decimal units and retained-original-byte accounting including revisions/admission reservations. Q45 adds **10 GB for lifetime-rights holders**, not guaranteed lifetime hosting; no general free tier. Eligible rights, duplicate claims, stacking/floor, paid-to-included continuity, introductory trials and prices/discounts remain open. Native checkout is required; Q54 permits native-only launch if web cannot qualify. Q39 grants paid Cloud-target manual/scheduled exports without another lifetime purchase, not unrelated local/direct premium access or agent authority. No source implements these public allowances yet.
- **Large JSON app exports:** Q43/Q46 reject an arbitrary whole-native-export cap within remaining allowance. Use bounded authenticated resumable upload, validation/encryption, indexing, reads, portability and erasure, not unlimited bodies/memory. Q47 selects JSON original artifacts; exact JSON schema families remain open, and raw/NDJSON support is not silently admitted. Preserve lossless fields/embedded binary, legacy API/pilot limits and shipped bytes; headers confer no app authority.
- **Public identity:** passkeys plus offline recovery codes; verified email for enrollment/notifications, not a recovery bypass. Loss of all authentication factors can leave an account inaccessible even if its archive survives.
- **Primary retention and retirement:** no age expiry under valid storage entitlement and no silent quota eviction. Q48 starts **30-day archive grace at verified end of paid access**, including applicable store grace—not cancellation/first failed charge. Q49 preserves downloads, existing MCP reads and security/deletion controls, not unentitled uploads. Q51 retires the archive/associated decryption authority and old grants, retaining minimal sign-in/billing/security/deletion-control state. Q52 requires email **and** persistent app/dashboard notices at days 0/7/21/29, holding irreversible retirement if Health.md cannot attempt required warnings. Q53 distinguishes verified pending/completed primary erasure from provider-copy expiry and external transcripts. Q45's included-capacity continuation remains open; never assume subscription expiry means loss of all storage rights. Existing finite revision expiry is not this lifecycle.
- **Downgrade and deliberate reduction:** Q50 requires users to delete enough stored data before a capacity downgrade and rejects the proposed automatic still-paid over-quota retirement. Qualify owner-selected deletion, reservations and pending changes. External store plan management prevents an assumed universal UI/backend veto; the safe fallback and deletion granularity await Q61–Q62. No forced billing or silent eviction.
- **No Health.md backups for the MVP.** Keep permanent-loss disclosure; do not build off-host recovery sets/key escrow or claim the original warning-retirement target achieved. Formal 15-minute/four-hour recovery targets remain declined. Q26 permits D1 automatic history subject to reviewed US location/retention/deletion/no-resurrection controls; this is not a selected D1 deployment or consistent archive/key recovery system. Other provider copies require their own review.
- **Exclusively US Health.md compute/storage is required for the MVP.** Verify TLS termination, all workloads, metadata, objects, keys and permitted provider copies. Self-attestation is selected, but the actual provider/topology and processor terms remain unqualified; no existing deployment is qualified as US-only.
- **Business assumption and responsibility:** Q56 **withdraws Q29/Q37's $100 spending envelope**. The owner expects profitability; that is not measured evidence, a public guarantee or permission to spend/provision. Review prices, fees, settlement timing, annual obligations and finite capacity without reinstating a dollar ceiling. Q33 assigns owner operations/support end to end; service/support promises and independent security/privacy/mobile reviewers remain separate. Quota/security/abuse safeguards and controlled admission are not waived.

These answers replace this backlog's original invite-beta/MCP-deferral recommendations. They do not authorize opening signup or changing the pilot.

Production readiness requires both software features and deployed operational evidence. A polished dashboard, encrypted disk, managed storage durability, green local restore test, or configuration flag is not sufficient individually.
