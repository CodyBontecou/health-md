## 1. TidBITS: strong topical fit, unusually strict pitch requirements

Adam Engst's [October 23, 2026 article](https://tidbits.com/2026/10/23/what-apple-health-data-revealed-about-my-cycling-crash/) describes needing selected Apple Health data, receiving a 255 MB archive, and working around a 4.7 GB XML file. That supports a focused export-workflow follow-up. It does not establish that Health.md can reproduce his medical investigation or that its daily summaries substitute for timestamped records.

The [official contact page](https://tidbits.com/about/contact/) names **ace@tidbits.com** for Adam's article pitches. Page undated; accessed October 27, 2026. It rejects guest/sponsored posts, link insertions, and press releases. It also explicitly discourages formulaic AI-written personalized app pitches and asks developers to establish unusual usefulness for its readership. A founder should edit the draft closely, show the concrete output, and invite independent testing. Do not ask to insert a link into the article or request a ranking-oriented anchor.

**Package:** selected-metric/date screenshot sequence, the actual fictional CSV, and the existing workbook preview/download. Link the task page, not only the homepage. Keep the story about portable files and inspectable output, without medical interpretation claims.

Clearly distinguish Health.md export from any optional visualization plugin and from an independently tested end-to-end setup. Keep scheduling caveats in the supporting guide; do not promise exact background run times.
